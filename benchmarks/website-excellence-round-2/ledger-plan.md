# Ledger — the round-2 validation app's build contract

> The benchmark scenario for the second Website Excellence round: a
> fintech treasury dashboard (the mission's PROJECT TYPE 2 — enterprise UX,
> data visualisation, dashboard design — the one gallery type never
> exercised), built UNDER the round's new capabilities: the
> `design-system-generator` skill generates the tokens before any
> component exists, the `motion-designer` and `frontend-craftsman` skills
> govern the build, and every iteration passes the craft axes of
> `scripts/design_critic.mjs` (schema 2). The design language is
> deliberately OPPOSITE to the landing's dark GBA aesthetic — warm paper,
> editorial serif, one ink, one accent (the Anthropic/Mercury lane of the
> research base) — proving the craft gates hold across design languages.

## The product fiction

"Ledger" — the treasury view of a fictional startup banking product
(the Mercury genre): cash position, runway, a cash-flow chart,
transactions across accounts, and a compliance footer. Every number is
seeded and deterministic; the receipts (the app's own test suite)
assert the displayed figures.

## The design system (generated by the design-system-generator procedure)

- **Voices (3):** a workhorse sans for UI (the system stack — this app
  has no brand-font budget and the rule is honesty: no fake Inter), a
  serif display for the numbers-as-headlines voice (Georgia/Times —
  the editorial contrast, Mercury's Tiempos lane), and a mono for
  figures and tabular data.
- **Type ramp (closed, 9 sizes):** 12/13/15/17px UI, 20/24/32px
  headings, 44px display, weights 400/500/600 ONLY (the craft ceiling).
- **Tracking:** display 44px at −0.02em; body 15-17px at 0; mono labels
  at +0.08em (the label voice, inside the ≤0.2em band).
- **Neutrals — the warm-paper law:** ONE hue (40° warm), 10 steps from
  `#faf9f5` (paper, Anthropic's own) to `#191817` (warm ink). Grays
  generated inside the hue; the tinted cluster must hold ≤30°.
- **Accent: exactly one** — a clay/terracotta `#c65f3d` with one
  interactive variant `#a84a2c` (the Anthropic clay lane), for links,
  the primary action, and the chart's key line ONLY. Semantic
  positive/negative for deltas: deep green `#3d6b4f` / deep red
  `#a34a3f` (data colors, not chrome).
- **Spacing:** base-4 scale (4→96), section padding 64/96, container
  1200px, reading width 680px.
- **Radius:** 4 (chips) / 8 (inputs) / 12 (cards) — small radii read
  tool-like (the Notion lane).
- **Breakpoints: 3** (744, 1068, 1440 — the Apple lane) — table →
  cards at 744, sidebar → stacked at 1068.
- **Motion:** compositing-only; one count-up on the KPI band (the
  numbers-as-headlines moment, finished-value-first in the DOM); the
  chart draws once via stroke-dashoffset (paint-only); hover lifts are
  translateY + border-color; reduced-motion renders everything
  finished. Zero JS animation libraries.

## The surface (one route, seven bands)

1. **Header** — wordmark (serif), 4 nav links, ONE filled action.
2. **The KPI band** — numbers as headlines (serif 44px): cash position,
   30-day net, runway months, annualized revenue — each with its delta
   chip and receipt line ("seeded fixture, Q4 FY26").
3. **The cash-flow chart** — 90-day SVG line chart (two lines: in/out,
   the accent on net), keyboard-operable range (native input), the
   legend, and an sr-only data table (the F17 lesson: the clip lives on
   a wrapper div, never the table).
4. **The transactions table** — 12 rows, merchant/amount/date/status,
   sortable by column (real buttons, aria-sort), the status chips on
   semantic colors, the row hover as a lift.
5. **The accounts strip** — 4 account cards (operating, payroll,
   reserve, tax), each with its bank-grade number row in mono.
6. **The compliance band** — Mercury's lane: the footnote apparatus as
   a designed section ("FDIC pass-through insurance", "Not a bank"),
   honest legalese, 13px, the quiet voice.
7. **Footer** — the fiction's honesty line ("a design fixture, not a
   bank") + the receipts link.

## The gates (all must pass on the final iteration)

- The critic's FULL rubric `--enforce`: every objective gate + every
  craft axis + the VLM seven axes ≥ 7.
- The app's component suite (bun test) — the drift-gate pattern: the
  seed's numbers asserted field-by-field.
- `bun run typecheck` + `lint` + `build` + the full pre-push battery.
- The e2e a11y pass on the route (axe, both schemes) where the
  playwright config covers it.
