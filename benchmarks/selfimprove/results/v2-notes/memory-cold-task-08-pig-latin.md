## Conventions
- Pig Latin: 'y' is a vowel only when it follows a consonant cluster, not at word start
- Pig Latin: treat 'qu' as a single unit when moving consonants to word end

## Known pitfalls
- When scanning for vowels, detect 'qu' before treating 'u' as a standalone vowel —
  'q' and 'u' must move together with preceding consonants
