## Conventions
- Use a set to track all students across grades to prevent duplicates.
- Use `sorted()` on both grades and student names to meet sorting requirements.

## Known pitfalls
- The `added()` method must track boolean results for each `add_student` call in order — do not aggregate or lose per-call results.
