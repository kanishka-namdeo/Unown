## The design deviations from the receipt (all operator-priced, receipted above)

1. The blob regex admits ONE nesting level (the receipt said
   "non-greedy, repeatable" — insufficient for the observed payload).
2. The JSON-fragment strip consumes the fragment's LEADING quote (the
   previous string's closer) — the receipt did not name it; the
   observed payload required it.
3. The Reason strip is sentence-start (lookbehind), not
   string-start-or-space — the receipt's "trailing Reason:/reason:
   clauses (the existing regex, generalized)" was ambiguous; the pins
   chose the conservative reading (mid-prose "reason" survives).
4. The whitespace-only floor short-circuits before the shape probes
   (byte-identity yields to the drop signal for empty rows).
