Plan gallery app #8: Encore — the Website Excellence showcase, the flagship
(the earned performance: what seven apps of practice taught the platform,
shown as one elite-grade web experience). It will live in-repo like its
siblings: src/lib/encore/ (pure domain), src/app/encore/ (the route), and
src/components/encore/ (UI), embedded on the landing at /encore.

Encore is a SHOWCASE, not a SaaS domain: its content is the Website
Excellence Program's own measured story — the critique baseline, the
iteration receipts, the tools built, the failures ledgered, the learning
retained. Every number on the page must trace to a committed receipt under
benchmarks/website-excellence-round/ (the landing truth rules: a figure with
no receipt is a lie, and this app is ABOUT the receipts).

The design bar is the round's research synthesis
(docs/website-excellence-research.md — READ IT FIRST, it is the design
brief): the craft baseline of Linear/Stripe/Vercel applied through the
Unown brand (warm paper/ink/gold, Game-Boy-inspired, dark-first — the
existing token system in src/app/globals.css, NOT a new palette).

   - Type ramp: EXACTLY 8 sizes max (e.g. 12/14/16/18/24/32/48/64px or the

   - Motion: 150/250/400ms budget, ease-out reveals, one interactive

     without status meaning; reveals settle to a base-finished state
     (blank-frame-under-reduced-motion is a defect the critic catches).
   - Layout: max-w container (the centered-layout rule), the section
     grammar, the grid per section.

5. The section-by-section UX spec — Encore's bands, each with its data
